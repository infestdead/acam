Camera mode: use a spare phone as the camera. Turn it on under Add camera → "Use this phone as a camera instead"; the viewing phone finds it on the same Wi-Fi and asks for its code, or you type its address (also over a VPN such as Tailscale). From the viewing phone you can switch between its back and selfie camera and turn on a light: the torch, with its strength where the phone allows, or with the selfie camera the phone's own screen.
